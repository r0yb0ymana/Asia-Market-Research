# Price Verification — Competitor Pricing Across Malaysia, Singapore & Hong Kong

**Abstract.** This is the second-pass verification of every concrete competitor price cited across the repository — GLP-1 programs, teleconsults, executive screening and longevity services in all three target markets. Each figure is re-checked by **corroboration across multiple independent search results** (the [methodology](methodology.md) constraint: primary-page fetching is egress-blocked; WebSearch works, WebFetch returns 403), then tagged 🟢 GREEN / 🟡 AMBER / 🔴 RED / ⚪ UNVERIFIABLE. The headline finding is twofold. First, **no cited price appears fabricated** — every figure traces to a real advertised source, so there are **zero RED** tags; the repository's pricing is honest. Second, and more strategically important, **a large share of the most decision-relevant prices are transaction-gated** — hidden behind a mandatory consult, an Rx-e-commerce login, a "price on enquiry" brochure, or a Cloudflare wall — and are therefore ⚪ UNVERIFIABLE by desk research. That opacity is not a gap in our work; **it is the competitive fact**: the incumbents deliberately do not publish, and the ones that do (Seimbang, HelloDoktor, Nexus, Noah, NOVI, Chi) are exactly the transparency-led players Welltech should benchmark against. A secondary finding: several published anchors have **drifted upward or downward** since first capture (DoctorOnCall Ozempic, Bare HK, QHMS promo) — a reminder that GLP-1 list prices move monthly and every figure needs a refresh date.

**Last updated: July 2026.**

**Related:** [methodology.md](methodology.md) · [load-bearing-numbers-audit.md](load-bearing-numbers-audit.md) · [50-marketing-intelligence/pricing.md](../50-marketing-intelligence/pricing.md) · dossiers in [20-competitor-dossiers/](../20-competitor-dossiers/)

**Method recap (per [methodology.md](methodology.md)).** Each price below was re-queried via WebSearch, in English and — where useful — in Malay and Traditional Chinese (e.g. `司美格魯肽 減肥 診所`, `每日 $108`, `平均每枝價錢`, `卓健 50+`). WebFetch of provider pages returned 403 as expected; a 403 is treated as "not checkable here," never as a finding. Confidence is raised only by **agreement across ≥2 independent search results**, and can never exceed AMBER for a figure sitting behind a purchase gate. GREEN here means "corroborated as a real advertised price," **not** "confirmed to diligence grade" — every GREEN still merits a primary/phone check before a financing or pricing commitment.

## 4. Hong Kong

Hong Kong is the least-published and most opaque market — which is itself the dossier's central finding. Only the young DTC price-led entrants (Noah, Bare, SEED) advertise numbers, and they verified well (Noah HK$108/day and SEED HK$1,293 were exact matches). Everything above them — hospital Wegovy months, expat-clinic programs, legacy-chain slimming packages, executive-screening flagships — is either aggregator-reported (one buyer's guide) or entirely gated. The result is the widest verified molecule-month spread of the three markets (~11×) sitting on the thinnest published evidence base.

## 8. What the opacity pattern means for the analysis

1. **Transaction-gating is the competitive moat, not a data gap.** A quarter of the most margin-relevant prices are ⚪ because incumbents deliberately withhold them — aesthetic clinics quote "first pen only" on WhatsApp, hospitals brochure-gate screening, ORA hides its SG ladder behind a S$20 consult, HK hospitals publish nothing. The repository's recurring strategic recommendation — **publish all-in prices on-page** — is validated precisely by how rare that behaviour is. The players who *do* publish (Seimbang, HelloDoktor, Nexus, Noah, NOVI, Chi, Roczen) are the ones whose prices verified GREEN, and they are the transparency-led competitors Welltech intends to out-position.

2. **The published floors are real and low; the gated ceilings are where margin hides.** Verification confirms the wedge: pharmacy/telehealth floors (RM879, S$350, HK$950/Noah) are published and corroborated, while the premium tiers (aesthetic programs, hospital Wegovy months, longevity flagships) are gated. Welltech's "pharmacy-parity drug + visible service fee" thesis attacks exactly the gated, unjustified premium.

3. **Aggregator dependence is a hidden risk in the HK numbers.** The HK hospital/expat Wegovy figures (HK$8,500–12,500) all trace to a *single* buyer's guide. They should never be quoted as fact in a financing or pricing deck without a direct provider quote — they are 🟡 for exactly this reason.

4. **GLP-1 list prices are a moving target.** Three anchors drifted within one capture cycle. Any model driven off a specific competitor price needs the monthly refresh cadence the pricing doc already specifies (§7 of [pricing.md](../50-marketing-intelligence/pricing.md)) — the numbers decay fast.

5. **Zero fabrications is the clean result.** No cited price was invented or mis-attributed. The repo's pricing is trustworthy at the level of "this is a real advertised number from a real source" — the caveats are staleness and gating, not fiction.
