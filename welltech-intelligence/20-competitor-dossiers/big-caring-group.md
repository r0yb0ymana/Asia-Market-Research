# BIG CARING Group — Competitor Dossier

BIG CARING Group Bhd (bigcaring.com.my) is Malaysia's largest pharmacy retail group: **626 outlets** under the BIG Pharmacy, CARiNG Pharmacy, Georgetown Pharmacy, Wellings and Ting Pharmacy brands, **FY2025 (June) revenue of RM3.41 billion and net profit of RM143 million**, roughly 11% of Malaysia's ~3,500 community pharmacies.[^1][^2][^3] The group was created by BIG Pharmacy Healthcare's 2023 acquisition of Caring Pharmacy Group from 7-Eleven Malaysia (Berjaya/Vincent Tan orbit) in a deal worth ~RM888M for 100%, backed by private-equity firm Creador. As of mid-2026 it has filed a draft prospectus for a Bursa Malaysia Main Market IPO seeking up to **RM3 billion (~USD750M) at a reported ~RM20 billion valuation**, with proceeds to pare RM1.3B of debt and fund an automated Klang distribution centre plus 40–50 new outlets/year.[^4][^5][^6] BIG CARING is a discount-retail scale machine with a stated ambition to become "Southeast Asia's integrated healthcare group" (primary care, digital health, tele-consultation, distribution, manufacturing) — but today its healthcare-services and WhatsApp capabilities remain thin relative to [Alpro](alpro-pharmacy.md). See also [doc2us.md](doc2us.md), [../10-market-intelligence/malaysia-whatsapp-healthcare.md](../10-market-intelligence/malaysia-whatsapp-healthcare.md) and [../10-market-intelligence/malaysia-weight-loss-market.md](../10-market-intelligence/malaysia-weight-loss-market.md).

### Lineage 1: BIG Pharmacy (2006–)

- **2006** — Founded by husband-and-wife pharmacists **Lee Meng Chuan** (BPharm, University of South Australia) and **Lim Sin Yin** (BPharm, University of Strathclyde); first outlet Damansara Uptown; positioning "the most affordable pharmacy chain in Malaysia".[^11][^12]
- **2018** — **Creador enters**: merges its RedCap Pharmacy chain into BIG Pharmacy; Creador stake later ~35% via Iris Pallida (Malay Mail dates Creador's first investment to 2015 — sources conflict on the year; the RedCap merger in 2018 is the well-documented event).[^10][^6]
- **2018–2023** — Aggressive organic expansion (100→150+ outlets) with discount-led, high-SKU front-shop retailing.[^11][^13]

### Lineage 2: CARiNG Pharmacy (1994–)

- **1994** — Founded by **five pharmacist course-mates from Universiti Sains Malaysia, led by Chong Yeow Siang** (MD); first outlet Taman Muda, Cheras; distinctive "pharmacist always on duty" professional format.[^14][^15]
- **Nov 2013** — Listed on Bursa Malaysia (Caring Pharmacy Group Bhd, stock 5245); Berjaya's Tan Sri Vincent Tan held ~20%.[^15][^16]
- **FY2019** — Revenue RM599.2M (+17.9% y/y), net profit RM25.6M, 125 outlets.[^17]
- **Dec 2019–May 2020** — **7-Eleven Malaysia takeover**: Convenience Shopping (Sabah) Sdn Bhd + parties acting in concert (7-Eleven Malaysia, Vincent Tan, Jitumaju, U Telemedia) bought 25.35% for RM143.51M, lifting concert-party holdings to 38.57% and triggering an MGO at **RM2.60/share, valuing Caring at ~RM566M**; delisted May 2020. (Reported acquisition-cost figures vary: MARC cites RM266.9M of mostly-borrowed funding in 2020; Fifth Person cites RM423.2M for the 75% position — treat as different tranche accounting of the same takeover.)[^18][^19][^16][^20]
- **Oct 2020** — Under 7-Eleven, Caring buys 67% of The Pill House (Georgetown Pharmacy) for RM25.51M and 60% of Wellings Pharmacy for RM19.9M — RM48.86M total, extending into Penang/northern region.[^21]
- **Jul 2023** — Unified Caring Membership across CARiNG, Georgetown, Wellings: "3 Great Pharmacies, 1 Awesome App", 250+ stores.[^22]

### The 2023 merger — verified deal values

- **21 Jul 2023** — 7-Eleven Malaysia agrees to sell its 75% of Caring to BIG Pharmacy for **RM637.5M**; press initially framed the whole-company deal at ~RM900M including the 25% minority.[^23][^4]
- **Sep 2023** — Price revised **+RM37.5M to RM675M** for the 75%.[^24]
- **29 Dec 2023** — Completion; MarketScreener records the 75% completion at **RM670M** (final adjusted consideration), plus acquisition of the remaining 25% from Motivasi Optima Sdn Bhd (Chong Yeow Siang and partners).[^8][^25]
- **Total consideration ~RM888.33M for 100% of Caring**, funded by an RM831.3M term loan — the debt the IPO is designed to repay.[^9][^5]

## 2. Business model

| Stream | Mechanics | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Retail pharmacy (core) | High-volume, price-led sales of pharmaceutical, healthcare, beauty and general merchandise across 626 outlets; "most affordable chain" positioning at BIG; SSSG 9.6% FY2025 | [^7][^11][^2] |
| Prescription dispensing | All brands dispense Rx incl. chronic meds; CARiNG's heritage format keeps a pharmacist on duty all opening hours; prescription share of revenue not disclosed in press coverage of the prospectus — lower than Alpro's as share of mix *(inference from front-shop-heavy formats)* | [^14][^7] |
| E-commerce | BIG eStore (bigpharmacy.com.my), CARiNG eStore (estore.caring2u.com; DHL eCommerce delivery 3–5 days West Malaysia, RM5 shipping rebate ≥RM100; Click & Collect at 50 stores within 3 hours), plus official Shopee/Lazada stores | [^31][^32][^33] |
| Membership/loyalty | BIG: free lifetime membership, 1 point/RM1 across BIG + Ting + online; app onboarding promo 600 points + RM5 off. CARiNG: 2.4M+ members, 1 point/RM1, 150 points = RM1, 3-tier structure, silver at Loyalty & Engagement Awards 2024 | [^34][^35][^22][^36] |
| Wholesale/manufacturing | Prospectus scope: manufacturing, importing, marketing, distributing and wholesaling healthcare, pharmaceutical and nutraceutical products (own-brand and third-party) | [^7] |
| Services | Physiotherapy, **tele-consultation** and other healthcare-related services listed in prospectus business description; in-store screening/vaccination campaigns at CARiNG | [^7][^37] |
| Corporate/panel | Corporate sales and panel supply exist across brands but are not a highlighted segment in IPO coverage *(press coverage gap — verify against final prospectus)* | [^7] |

**GLP-1 dispensing:** No Ozempic/Wegovy/Rybelsus/Saxenda listings were found on bigpharmacy.com.my (site-restricted search returned zero results, July 2026).[^38] Third-party access guides nonetheless name BIG Pharmacy alongside Alpro as retail chains dispensing GLP-1s against a valid prescription "often at the most competitive retail rates" (semaglutide is a Group B Poison; pens retail ~RM850–1,150).[^39][^40] Net: BIG CARING fulfils GLP-1 demand quietly in-store but — unlike [Alpro](alpro-pharmacy.md) — does not merchandise it online and has no visible weight-management program or dietitian-led GLP-1 support.

## 3. Healthcare-services expansion

- **Stated strategy:** "expanding beyond pharmacy as Southeast Asia's integrated healthcare group across primary care, B2B and B2C, digital health, distribution, manufacturing" (corporate site) — ambition is explicit, execution is early.[^3]
- **In-store services:** CARiNG outlets run health screening, chronic-disease management support, vaccination services and health campaigns across 200+ outlets; BIG offers dietitian/nutritionist advice in selected stores.[^37][^31]
- **Telehealth:** the BIG Pharmacy app advertises "online consultations with certified doctors"; the group is a DOC2US partner-pharmacy network member (e-prescriptions dispensed at BIG outlets; DOC2US kiosk model), i.e. doctor capacity is rented, not owned.[^41][^42]
- **Physiotherapy & tele-consultation** appear as service lines in the prospectus business description — the clearest signal the IPO story includes services revenue, not just retail.[^7]
- **Regional preventive-health alliance:** B&S Corporation (Japan) + Great Tree Pharmacy (Taiwan) partnership (Dec 2025) targets preventive-healthcare formats and cross-border product/knowledge flows.[^27][^28]
- **No clinics, no in-house doctors, no chronic-care subscription products** were found in press or on group sites as of July 2026 — the services layer is aspirational relative to Alpro's clinic/nutritionist bench. *(observation)*

## 4. WhatsApp usage — detail for Welltech

- **BIG Pharmacy:** home delivery is explicitly ordered by **"WhatsApp, SMS or call-in to any BIG Pharmacy outlet near you"** — customers contact their local store (per-outlet numbers via the store locator), provide name/contact/address, and the outlet arranges dispatch; Facebook posts push "WhatsApp order now to any BIG Pharmacy outlet".[^43][^44][^45]
- **CARiNG:** "WhatsApp your nearby CARiNG store for help" is the official assist channel; e-commerce runs through estore.caring2u.com (DHL, Click & Collect) rather than chat-to-order.[^32][^33]
- **Structure:** WhatsApp is **decentralised to ~600 individual store numbers** — no single national pharmacist line, no published chronic-refill WhatsApp workflow, no WhatsApp Business API/automation evident. This contrasts with Alpro's single advertised pharmacist line (+60 19-702 1923) and Klang Valley 2-hour flow.[^43][^32]
- **Assessment:** BIG CARING treats WhatsApp as a store-level convenience channel, not an operated care channel. Its 2.4M+ CARiNG members and 626-store footprint mean a centralised WhatsApp refill program would be instantly massive if launched — a key strategic watch-item. See [../10-market-intelligence/malaysia-whatsapp-healthcare.md](../10-market-intelligence/malaysia-whatsapp-healthcare.md).

## 5. Technology & AI

- **Supply chain:** Apr-2026 Klang DC is the group's technology showpiece — 160+ robots, tote-to-person/shelf-to-person systems, AI demand-forecast replenishment across the network, 90,000+ order lines/day, 99.8% accuracy; built because the Bukit Raja facility would hit capacity within five years at 40–50 new stores/year.[^29][^30]
- **Apps:** **BIG Pharmacy app** (Google Play `my.com.bigpharmacy`; iOS id1473583490) — membership, health tracking, e-commerce, doctor tele-consults; **Caring Membership app** — unified loyalty across three brands, 2.4M+ members, 60% download growth post-integration, NPS 24%→26% (2022→2023). Published star-ratings were not verifiable from fetched sources — flag for direct app-store check.[^41][^46][^22][^36]
- **Consumer-facing AI:** none found. Technology investment is concentrated in logistics and loyalty, mirroring the group's retail DNA. *(observation)*

## 6. Marketing & positioning

- **Price-promotion culture:** BIG's brand promise is affordability; both banners run continuous promotions, member days ("Carnival" events), Shopee/Lazada flash sales — the group trains consumers to buy health products on discount.[^11][^33][^36]
- **Social reach (July 2026):** BIG Pharmacy ~112K Facebook likes, ~11K Instagram; CARiNG ~276K Facebook followers, ~67K Instagram — the largest social footprint among Malaysian pharmacy chains, an order of magnitude above Alpro's IG.[^47][^48]
- **Loyalty as moat:** CARiNG's award-winning unified membership (2.4M+) is the group's deepest consumer asset; BIG's points now span Ting Pharmacy too.[^22][^34]
- **Content/SEO:** health articles exist on brand sites but are thinner and more commerce-led than Alpro's clinical library; the IPO narrative (Bloomberg, The Edge, The Star) is currently generating most brand press.[^6][^5]

## 7. Reviews & reputation

- **Customers:** outlet Google reviews are mixed-to-positive and price-anchored *(directional; not systematically compiled here)*; a third-party aggregator reports high (4.5-type) satisfaction for the MY chain but is low-reliability. Note: Trustpilot complaints for "big-pharmacy.org" concern an unrelated foreign website, not the Malaysian chain — a brand-confusion risk BIG inherits.[^49]
- **Employees:** Glassdoor/Indeed reviews cite messy post-merger management, non-standardised SOPs, weak onboarding, low pay for degree-holders and **"very pushy on sales... individual sales target is high"** — sales-target culture is even more pronounced than at Alpro.[^50][^51]
- **Governance:** transition from founder/PE ownership to public-market scrutiny (2026 IPO) will force disclosure quality up; RM1.3B leverage is the stated investor concern in press coverage.[^2][^5]

## 8. Strengths / weaknesses

**Strengths**
1. Unmatched scale: 626 outlets, RM3.41B revenue, ~11% market share, five banners covering discount-to-professional formats and East Malaysia.[^1][^2][^3]
2. IPO warchest: up to RM3B raise enables debt paydown plus 40–50 outlets/year and services M&A.[^5][^6]
3. Best-in-class supply chain (automated Klang DC) — structural cost advantage in medication fulfilment.[^29]
4. 2.4M+ member loyalty base and biggest social reach in the category.[^22][^47]
5. PE/institutional discipline (Creador) and proven M&A execution (RedCap, Caring, Georgetown, Wellings, Ting).[^10][^9]

**Weaknesses**
1. Retail DNA and discount positioning undermine premium clinical-service credibility; pushy-sales reputation.[^11][^50]
2. No doctors, no clinics, no owned telehealth — clinical layer rented from DOC2US-type partners.[^42][^41]
3. WhatsApp fragmented across store numbers; no centralised chronic-refill or care workflows.[^43][^32]
4. Post-merger integration strain (SOPs, systems, five brands) plus RM1.3B leverage constrain near-term services investment.[^50][^2]
5. No visible GLP-1/weight-management program despite fulfilment capability — category passivity.[^38][^39]

| | Helpful | Harmful |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal** | Scale, price leadership, automated logistics, loyalty base, multi-banner coverage, IPO capital | No prescriber layer; fragmented WhatsApp; integration debt; discount brand ceiling for medical services; leverage |
| **External** | GLP-1/preventive-health demand growth; ageing chronic population; IPO-funded services M&A; Japan/Taiwan preventive-format partners | Alpro's professional-services lead; DTC telehealth (DoctorOnCall, DOC2US) owning the prescription origin; margin erosion from price wars; public-market scrutiny of thin services margins |

## 10. Threat assessment

**Threat level to Welltech: MEDIUM today, HIGH post-IPO.** BIG CARING owns the two assets that decide long-run chronic/weight-care economics — fulfilment (626 outlets + automated DC) and footfall (millions of loyalty members) — making it structurally the most dangerous potential entrant into subscription chronic care. But its DNA is discount retail: no doctors, no program design, no centralised care channel, and management attention through 2026–2027 is consumed by IPO, integration and debt reduction. The realistic threat path is **acquisition or partnership post-IPO** (e.g., buying a telehealth provider or launching a GLP-1 program with a partner-prescriber) rather than organic build. The Dec-2025 Great Tree/B&S preventive-health alliance and the prospectus's "tele-consultation" service line show the intent is already documented. *(analyst judgment)*[^7][^27]

## 11. Implications for Welltech — compete vs partner

1. **Near-term: exploit the services gap.** For 12–24 months BIG CARING will out-discount everyone but cannot out-care anyone. Welltech's doctor-led, WhatsApp-native weight/longevity programs face no equivalent from the group today — win the category narrative before their IPO capital is deployed into services.
2. **Partnership option — fulfilment breadth vs Alpro's depth.** BIG CARING offers more doors (626) and a superior DC for nationwide medication logistics; Alpro offers deeper clinical services and a proven single-line WhatsApp flow. For GLP-1 cold-chain and program fulfilment, Alpro is the better-fit first partner; BIG CARING is the scale option once Welltech volumes justify multi-chain distribution. *(analysis)*
3. **Their loyalty base is a customer-acquisition channel.** A co-branded screening-to-program funnel (CARiNG members → Welltech assessment → GLP-1/longevity program → dispense at CARiNG) would monetise their footfall without Welltech building retail — the AIA/Alpro screening tie-up is the template.[^37]
4. **Watch triggers:** (a) final IPO prospectus disclosure of tele-consultation/physiotherapy revenue and any "digital health" use-of-proceeds; (b) acquisition of a telehealth/clinic asset post-listing; (c) centralisation of WhatsApp ordering or a chronic-refill subscription; (d) online GLP-1 merchandising or a weight-management program launch; (e) Great Tree-style preventive formats appearing in Malaysian stores.[^5][^7][^27]
5. **Pricing gravity:** BIG's discount culture will compress retail margins on GLP-1s and supplements; Welltech should avoid molecule-margin dependence and anchor pricing on supervision, outcomes and continuity — the parts a discount retailer cannot replicate. See [../10-market-intelligence/malaysia-weight-loss-market.md](../10-market-intelligence/malaysia-weight-loss-market.md).
6. **Talent/ops caution:** both big chains show sales-KPI cultures in employee reviews; Welltech's pharmacist- and coach-facing propositions can differentiate on professional autonomy when recruiting from these networks.[^50][^47]
