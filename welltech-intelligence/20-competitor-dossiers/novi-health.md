# Competitor Dossier: NOVI Health

**Abstract.** NOVI Health (novi-health.com; NOVI Health Pte Ltd) is Singapore's leading metabolic-health and medical weight-loss clinic and the closest Singapore analogue to Welltech's clinical model. Founded in 2018 by three doctors — Dr Sue-Anne Toh (CEO & Medical Director, ex-NUH senior consultant endocrinologist), Dr Kyle Tan (Chief Product & Business Development Officer) and Dr Joanne Tay — it pairs a single specialist clinic (160 Robinson Road) with a proprietary app (NOVIFY) delivering doctor + dietitian + fitness-coach programmes: NOVI Optimum (lifestyle, from ~S$99–108/month), NOVI Optimum Plus (GLP-1-inclusive medical weight loss, from S$430/month or S$1,289/3 months incl. GST), NOVI Magnum (diabetes, from S$359/3 months, Medisave/CHAS-claimable) and NOVI Max (longevity, 100+ biomarkers incl. the NUS LinAge2 biological-age clock). It raised a US$5M Series A led by Monk's Hill Ventures (Nov 2022), publishes peer-reviewed outcomes — most importantly a 708-patient International Journal of Obesity study (2026, with NUS) showing 12.7% mean weight loss at 12 months and 14.7% at 18 months — and in June 2026 signed a strategic partnership with Novo Nordisk Singapore. Google rating ~4.9★. Its weaknesses are scale (~34 staff, one clinic, Singapore-only) and premium pricing; its evidence base, pharma alliance and longevity extension make it the reference competitor — and the highest-value partnership or acquisition target — for Welltech in Singapore.

## 2. Business model & revenue streams

NOVI monetises **programmes, not consults**: subscription-style bundled care with medication, diagnostics and retail attached. Revenue streams:

1. **B2C programmes** (core): Optimum, Optimum Plus, Magnum, Max — recurring monthly/quarterly billing.[^9][^10][^11][^12]
2. **Medication margin inside Optimum Plus** (GLP-1 dispensing bundled into programme fees).[^10]
3. **Diagnostics & screening**: HbA1c, biomarker panels (100+ markers in Max), VO₂ max, body composition; health screening services.[^12][^26]
4. **Retail/subscription store**: CGM sensors (Dexcom G7, Abbott FreeStyle Libre 2 Plus, BUZUD), glucometers, supplements — with monthly/quarterly auto-ship.[^11]
5. **B2B employers**: Optimum (weight) and Magnum (chronic disease) deployed as employee benefits; because NOVI contracts as a healthcare provider, costs are often billable through medical claims and in many cases claimable via the Health Promotion Board.[^27]
6. **Public-sector/institutional**: SAF programme precedent.[^20]
7. **Pharma partnership**: Novo Nordisk alliance (structure undisclosed — likely co-funded patient-support/coaching rather than revenue share; *(inference)*).[^7][^24]
8. **Payer integration**: Medisave-claimable (85% for Magnum bundle with 15% co-pay) and CHAS-accredited (S$28–90 offset per visit by tier) for chronic disease care — rare among startup clinics and a genuine affordability moat for diabetes.[^26]

## 4. Clinical model & end-to-end workflow

### 4.1 The multidisciplinary programme design

Each patient gets a **care team**: specialist doctor (endocrinology-led practice) + dietitian + fitness coach, coordinated through NOVIFY. The published Optimum Plus model: physician-directed GLP-1 therapy coupled with personalised diet and physical-activity counselling by a dedicated care team, with continuous support through the proprietary app.[^10][^22][^28]

**Cadence** *(assembled from programme pages and study description)*:

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| Onboarding | In-clinic or video first appointment: doctor consult + dietitian consult; baseline labs (HbA1c for Magnum; biomarker panel for Max); CGM fitted where indicated[^12][^26] |
| Weeks 1–12 | App-based coaching: food/activity/sleep logging with dietitian & coach feedback; CGM data streaming; GLP-1 initiation and titration under doctor direction (Optimum Plus)[^10][^21][^22] |
| Reviews | Doctor reviews every 1 or 3 months (plan-dependent, video or in-person); coach/dietitian messaging continuous[^10][^21] |
| Renewal | Monthly/quarterly billing; medication dispensed per review; store cross-sell (CGM, supplements)[^9][^10][^11] |

### 4.2 NOVIFY / NOVI Health app

- Tracks physiological variables (glucose, blood pressure, weight) and behaviour (diet, physical activity, sleep, medication); integrates wearables and CGMs so "all health data are in one place"; goal-setting set by the care team; progress visualised in charts; **1:1 remote digital coaching** via continuous messaging with coach and doctor reviews.[^21]
- Member web portal (member.novihealth.com) complements iOS/Android apps.[^21]
- Engagement is not cosmetic: the IJO study quantifies that high app-engagers achieved 2.0–2.2 percentage points more weight loss (~15% greater relative loss) than low engagers — NOVI has turned its app-engagement gradient into published evidence.[^22][^23]

### 4.3 Outcome tracking & publications — the moat

NOVI is the only Singapore weight-loss operator with a peer-reviewed real-world outcomes programme:

1. **JMIR Diabetes (2021)** — SAF partnership, single-group pre–post study of the mHealth-anchored programme for T2D/prediabetes during COVID-19; feasibility, acceptability and glycaemic-control evaluation.[^20]
2. **International Journal of Obesity (2026), with NUS Saw Swee Hock School of Public Health** — 708 adults in NOVI Optimum Plus, 6–18 months: **mean weight loss 12.7% at 12 months (95% CI −14.0, −11.3), BMI −4.1 points; 14.7% at 18 months** — on par with RCTs and well above typical real-world GLP-1 outcomes; engagement dose-response (+2.0–2.2pp); multi-ethnic cohort (East Asian 45.5%, European 26.8%, South Asian 13.3%, Southeast Asian 10.3%; mean age 42.1; 64.1% female).[^22][^23]
3. **ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07444073 (2026, NUS-sponsored)** — biological aging (LinAge2) in a real-world medical weight-loss programme; not yet recruiting at registration; est. Feb–Dec 2026. This connects the weight franchise to the longevity franchise with a measurable aging endpoint.[^25]

So the answer to "does NOVI publish outcome data?" is **yes — peer-reviewed, NUS-co-authored, and PR-amplified**, with a registered longevity follow-on.[^20][^22][^23][^25]

### 4.4 Doctor model

Specialist clinic model with **employed/in-house clinicians** (named doctors: Dr Sue-Anne Toh; Dr Kyle Tan; patient reviews name "Dr Todd", "Dr Su Ann"), plus dietitians and coaches on staff (~34 headcount total) — the antithesis of gig-GP telehealth. Continuity of named clinician is the product.[^5][^6][^14][^16] *(headcount split doctor/coach not disclosed — gap)*

## 5. Regulatory posture

- **Licensing:** operates as a specialist outpatient medical clinic (with telemedicine delivery of consults) under Singapore's HCSA regime; its **Medisave/CHAS accreditation** for chronic disease management is itself evidence of MOH-recognised clinic status. No MOH enforcement action was found this cycle.[^26] *(inference from accreditations; verification of absence)*
- **GLP-1 advertising:** Singapore bars consumer advertising of prescription-only medicines. NOVI's public funnel is compliance-shaped: education-library articles ("Why weight loss medications don't always work as expected"), programme pages that market the *programme* (doctor + coaching) rather than the drug, and outcome-study PR — the same pattern Welltech must adopt. Only Wegovy and Saxenda are HSA-approved for weight loss; NOVI's study language covers GLP-1 RA pharmacotherapy generally.[^22][^29][^31]
- **Sector context:** MOH's Dec 2024 teleconsult-lapse scrutiny targeted MC/prescription mills; NOVI's in-person-anchored, specialist, programme-based model sits at the low-risk end of that spectrum.[^32]

## 10. Strengths / Weaknesses / SWOT

### Strengths
1. **Published, NUS-co-authored outcomes** (12.7%/12mo; 14.7%/18mo) — unique sales asset vs every SG competitor.[^22][^23]
2. Clinician-founder credibility (NUHS/SPHERiC pedigree) enabling institutional deals (SAF, NUS, Novo Nordisk).[^7][^14][^20][^25]
3. Full-stack metabolic platform: programmes + app + CGM retail + diagnostics + employer channel with Medisave/CHAS plumbing.[^9][^10][^11][^26][^27]
4. Longevity extension (NOVI Max + LinAge2) positions it for the next demand wave with a measurement moat.[^12][^25]
5. Pharma alliance: Novo Nordisk partnership converts the GLP-1 supplier into a demand channel.[^7][^24]
6. Reputation: ~4.9★ with continuity-of-care praise.[^5][^33]

### Weaknesses
1. Scale: ~34 staff, one clinic, Singapore-only; thousands (not hundreds of thousands) of patients.[^6][^7]
2. Capitalisation: US$5M Series A (2022) is thin against category marketing costs; no follow-on disclosed.[^1][^19]
3. Premium pricing exposed to medication-only discounters and to GLP-1 price deflation.[^29][^30]
4. App-walled-garden onboarding friction; no WhatsApp-native journey.[^21] *(verification of absence)*
5. Owned-social reach tiny (~3.1k IG); funnel dependent on SEO/PR/pharma referral.[^13]
6. Founder-dependence: medical brand concentrated in Dr Toh/Dr Tan. *(inference)*

## 11. Threat assessment for Welltech

1. **NOVI is the benchmark, not (yet) the blocker.** It validates Welltech's exact thesis — multidisciplinary GLP-1 + CGM + coaching with outcome tracking — in Singapore, at premium pricing, with published results Welltech will be compared against. Any Welltech Singapore launch must match or beat 12.7%/12-month outcomes messaging or reframe the metric. [^22]
2. **Does NOVI expand to Malaysia?** No announced plans found this cycle; Series A language and Monk's Hill framing invoke Southeast Asia's chronic-disease burden, and the ~100-nationalities patient base suggests medical-tourism pull, but headcount, capital and single-site operations point to SG consolidation first. **Probability of MY entry within 24 months: low-moderate (~25–35%)** — rising sharply if it raises a Series B or if Novo Nordisk regionalises the partnership. *(analyst estimate)*[^1][^7][^18]
3. **The Novo Nordisk alliance is the strategic event.** If Novo makes NOVI its structured-care reference partner across APAC, NOVI gets pharma-funded distribution Welltech cannot match organically; conversely it may lock NOVI to semaglutide economics just as tirzepatide takes share.[^7][^24] *(watch)*
4. **Partnership/acquisition angles:** (a) NOVI is the highest-value acquisition target in Welltech's SG universe — evidence base, payer accreditations, care-team playbooks, LinAge2 longevity IP access; likely expensive relative to revenue post-Novo deal; (b) partnership alternative: Welltech Malaysia as NOVI's regional delivery partner (their evidence + Welltech's WhatsApp-first ops); (c) talent/playbook: NOVI's programme design (review cadence, engagement-outcome instrumentation) is replicable — Welltech should instrument engagement from day one to build an equivalent dataset.
5. **Signals to watch:** Series B; second clinic or MY/ID entity registrations; Novo partnership expansion; NOVI Max pricing publication; AI-coaching launch; NCT07444073 results (would create the first weight-loss-to-biological-age outcomes claim in SEA).[^25]

## 12. Implications for Welltech

1. **Match the evidence architecture, differentiate the channel.** NOVI wins on published outcomes inside an app walled garden; Welltech should replicate the outcomes-instrumentation discipline (engagement gradients, ethnicity-stratified results) but deliver it WhatsApp-native, where onboarding friction disappears — NOVI's own data proves engagement drives outcomes, and WhatsApp maximises engagement. [^21][^22]
2. **Price against the S$430 anchor.** NOVI has established that S$430/month all-in (doctor+coaching+medication pathway) clears in Singapore. Welltech Malaysia can anchor materially lower (MYR cost base) while quoting the same programme architecture; in any SG entry, undercutting NOVI on price while matching cadence is viable given its premium positioning.[^10][^29]
3. **Copy the payer plumbing early.** Medisave/CHAS accreditation (SG) and employer medical-claims billability made NOVI's chronic-care line defensible; Welltech's MY equivalent is panel-clinic status and employer/insurer claimability — build it before marketing scale-up.[^26][^27]
4. **CGM as retail subscription, not just clinical add-on.** NOVI's store shows CGM monetising standalone at S$156–567/cycle with auto-ship; Welltech should structure CGM identically (programme component + DTC subscription).[^11]
5. **Longevity: measure, don't vibe.** NOVI Max + LinAge2 shows the winning longevity formula is a *quantified aging endpoint* attached to an existing metabolic franchise — precisely Welltech's planned sequence; a biological-age clock partnership (or equivalent) should be scoped for MY/HK.[^12][^25]
6. **Pre-empt the pharma channel in Malaysia.** Novo Nordisk (and Lilly) will seek structured-care partners in every SEA market; Welltech should open that conversation for Malaysia before a NOVI-style local analogue does.[^7][^24]
7. **Monitor quarterly:** novi-health.com pricing-page diffs, ACRA filings, MY/ID entity registrations, Novo announcements, NCT07444073 status, app-store rating trajectories.
