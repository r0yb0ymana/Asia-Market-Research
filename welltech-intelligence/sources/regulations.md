# Regulatory & Legal Instrument Index — Malaysia · Singapore · Hong Kong

A consolidated quick-reference index of every regulatory and legal instrument cited across the repository for Welltech Health's three markets. Instruments are grouped by market, then by compliance domain (telemedicine · prescribing & pharmacy · GLP-1 / controlled drugs · advertising · privacy · facility licensing · reimbursement · AI/SaMD). For each: the instrument, its jurisdiction/regulator, what it governs, a one-line implication for Welltech, and links to the primary source plus the repository document that analyses it.

**Last updated: July 2026**

The deep analysis lives in [malaysia-regulations.md](../10-market-intelligence/malaysia-regulations.md) and in the regulatory sections of [singapore-market-intelligence.md](../10-market-intelligence/singapore-market-intelligence.md) (§4) and [hong-kong-market-intelligence.md](../10-market-intelligence/hong-kong-market-intelligence.md) (§4). Full citations are in [bibliography.md](bibliography.md); all URLs in [links.md](links.md).

> **Caveat.** Several primary statute/guideline PDFs were accessed via search-indexed content rather than direct download (official hosts blocked retrieval). Dates, penalty figures and registration statuses should be re-verified against the primary URL before external use. Law-firm alerts are cited alongside primary sources as corroboration.

## One-screen cross-market map

| Dimension | Malaysia | Singapore | Hong Kong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Telehealth service licence | None enforced (Telemedicine Act 1997 dormant); OHS Guideline 2025 is soft law | **Required** — HCSA Outpatient Medical Service licence + Clinical Governance Officer | **None** — MCHK ethical guidelines only |
| Who bears regulatory risk | Doctor (MMC) + facility (PHFSA) | Licensee entity + doctor | **The individual doctor** (registration/discipline) |
| Rx-medicine control | Poisons Act 1952 (pharmacist dispensing) | Health Products Act / Poisons Act — POM class | Pharmacy & Poisons Ordinance (Part 1 poisons) |
| GLP-1 DTC advertising | MAB/KKLIU pre-approval regime | Banned for POMs, **actively enforced** | **UMAO statutory prohibition + Schedule 4 weight-claim limits** |
| Privacy | PDPA 2010 + 2024 amendments (in force 2025) | PDPA + healthcare advisory guidelines; NEHR mandate incoming | PDPO (Cap 486); **s.33 cross-border restriction not in force** |
| Practical entry friction | Low | High (licence + inspection) | **Lowest** — but discipline/press risk concentrated on doctors |
