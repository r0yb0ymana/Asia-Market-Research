# Master Annotated Bibliography — Welltech Intelligence Repository

The consolidated, deduplicated source register for the Welltech Health market-intelligence repository (digital healthcare across Malaysia, Singapore and Hong Kong). Sources are organised by type in the priority order set out in [RESEARCH-STANDARDS.md](../RESEARCH-STANDARDS.md): government and regulators first, then intergovernmental bodies, academic and clinical literature, company filings, market-research firms, trade press, and finally company/provider and digital sources. Each entry gives the source, its publisher, and a one-line annotation of what it supports and which repository folder(s) cite it.

**Last updated: July 2026**

**Scope.** ~76 documents across folders `00-executive-summary` through `70-welltech-blueprint` were harvested for their `## References` sections and inline citations, yielding ~3,130 unique URLs across ~1,250 domains. This bibliography curates the ~270 most load-bearing sources; the full URL list is in [links.md](links.md). Folder tags used below: `00`=executive-summary, `10`=market-intelligence, `20`=competitor-dossiers, `30`=patient-reviews, `40`=doctor-experience, `50`=marketing-intelligence, `60`=ai-operating-model, `70`=welltech-blueprint.

> **Methodology caveat (applies throughout).** Many government and regulator documents — particularly Malaysian ministry PDFs (moh.gov.my, pharmacy.moh.gov.my, mmc.gov.my, npra.gov.my) and several Singapore isomer-hosted circulars — were accessed via search-indexed content rather than direct download, because official hosts blocked direct retrieval in the research environment. Where a figure or date rests on search-extracted content, the citing document flags it (and often corroborates with a law-firm alert or press report). Treat statute dates, prices and registration statuses as re-verifiable against the primary URL before external use.
